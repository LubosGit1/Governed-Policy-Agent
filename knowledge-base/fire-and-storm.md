# Fire and Storm

Direct physical loss caused by accidental fire, lightning or explosion is covered up to the relevant building or contents limit on the schedule. Smoke damage resulting from a covered fire is treated as fire damage.

Storm damage is covered when wind causes direct physical damage to the insured property. Rain entering through an opening created by the same storm may be covered. Flood, surface water and groundwater are not storm damage and are subject to separate cover if shown on the schedule.
