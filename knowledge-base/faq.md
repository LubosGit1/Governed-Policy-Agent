# Frequently Asked Questions

**Is every water leak covered?** No. Sudden accidental escape from a fixed domestic system may be covered; gradual leaks and maintenance issues are excluded. The full wording and schedule control.

**Does an estimate confirm my claim will be paid?** No. A calculation using a limit, deductible and damage amount is illustrative only. The claims team assesses coverage, evidence and the final amount.

**What if I cannot find the answer here?** The available summaries may not include every contract condition. Ask the claims team or request a human review rather than relying on an assumption.
