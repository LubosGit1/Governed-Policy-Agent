# Limits and Deductible

The policy schedule lists the maximum building and contents amounts insured. The applicable limit is the amount shown for the damaged property and type of loss; optional endorsements can change those amounts.

For each accepted claim, the policyholder pays the deductible shown on the schedule. The insurer's payment cannot exceed the covered, assessed damage, the applicable limit, or zero after the deductible is applied. Multiple limits or deductibles may apply under the full contract.
