# Personal Liability

Personal liability cover, when shown on the schedule, may respond if an insured person becomes legally liable for accidental bodily injury or property damage to another person. The applicable limit and conditions are stated in the schedule and contract.

The policy does not cover intentional injury, contractual liability beyond what the law imposes, or liability arising from motor vehicles. Report an incident promptly and do not admit liability or agree a settlement without the insurer's written consent.
