# Making a Claim

Report a new claim as soon as reasonably possible using the Nordlight claims portal or the claims telephone service listed on the policy schedule. Provide the policy reference, date and description of the incident, and an estimate of the damage.

Keep damaged items where safe, take photographs, and retain invoices and receipts. Do not admit liability or dispose of evidence before the claims team advises you. The claims team may request more information or arrange an inspection.

Coverage and payment are decided only after an individual claim assessment under the full policy. An assistant estimate is informational and is not an approval or settlement offer.
