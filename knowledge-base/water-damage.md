# Escape of Water

Sudden and accidental escape of water from a fixed domestic pipe, heating system, appliance or water tank is covered, subject to the policy schedule and deductible. The damaged building or contents must belong to the insured interest.

Damage caused gradually by leakage, damp, condensation, corrosion, poor maintenance or repeated seepage is not covered. The policy does not pay to repair the failed pipe or appliance unless the schedule specifically includes that cover.
